# Austin Public Library

City of Austin public library system. First-party site: [library.austintexas.gov](https://library.austintexas.gov).

Storytime programs for ages 5 and under are listed at [Events → Storytimes](https://library.austintexas.gov/events/storytimes). The public site is a browseable HTML calendar; it does not publish an iCal / webcal feed (checked 2026-08-24).

Holiday closures for all locations are posted at the [Holiday Closure Schedule](https://library.austintexas.gov/node/1734929). In the Fall 2026 storytime window that includes Labor Day Sunday–Monday 6–7 September 2026 and Veterans Day Wednesday 11 November 2026.

ADA accommodations contact on storytime materials: 512-974-7400.
