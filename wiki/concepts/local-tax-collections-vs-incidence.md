# Local tax collections vs incidence

**Collections** = tax revenue recorded by governments where the remittance is booked.  
**Incidence** = who ultimately bears the economic burden.

Metro “tax per person” built from Census local finance files is **collections ÷ resident population**. It is not a household tax bill. Commuters, tourists, businesses, and cross-border shopping all move remittances away from residence.

When communicating metro tax metrics, always dimension-tag: *local tax collections / resident*.
