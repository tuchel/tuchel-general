# Fiscally standardized local finance (metro rollup)

Comparing **municipal budgets alone** understates the local public sector. Counties, independent school districts, and special districts often spend more than city hall.

## BLV / project method

For the Metro Budget & Tax Explorer, every local government unit in the Census State & Local Government Finances individual-unit file is assigned to an OMB CBSA via the unit’s home county FIPS, then summed and divided by CBSA population.

Lincoln Institute **FiSC** solves a related problem for ~212 large *central cities* by allocating overlying governments onto the city. This project’s unit of analysis is the **metro**, not the central city — FiSC remains the pedagogical contrast for “city hall only.”
