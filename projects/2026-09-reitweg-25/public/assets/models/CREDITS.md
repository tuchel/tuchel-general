# Car models

Both are from Sketchfab, shown in the garage in the Detailed and Extreme presets. Each was compressed with
glTF-Transform (meshopt geometry, WebP textures at up to 1,024 px), scaled in the viewer to its car's published length,
turned to face into the garage, and shown without the flat ground props it was exported with (a baked shadow, a name
plate). Colours and shapes are as modelled.

- `tesla-model-y-performance.glb`: "2026 Tesla Model Y Performance" by BloxBloger
  (https://sketchfab.com/BloxBloger), https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/2026-tesla-model-y-performance-8a0bc252f9da4015a02a7bd99efd4847,
  licensed CC BY-NC 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/): non-commercial use, with credit.
  Scaled to 4,796 mm.
- `porsche-911-turbo-1975.glb`: "FREE 1975 Porsche 911 (930) Turbo" by Lionsharp Studios
  (https://sketchfab.com/lionsharp), https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/free-1975-porsche-911-930-turbo-8568d9d14a994b9cae59499f0dbed21e,
  licensed CC BY 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Scaled to the 930 Turbo's 4,291 mm.
