# Prior Art — CONSTELLATION ZERO

**Job statement:** Build a mobile-first, browser-playable, *Metal Gear Solid* × *Metal Slug* side-scrolling stealth-action shooter where an astronaut travels to orbit in a starship to dismantle a rogue network of AI data-center satellites.

| Match | Link | Job | Gap vs. this project |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Star Mind** *(internal repo)* | [`../2026-07-star-mind/README.md`](../2026-07-star-mind/README.md) | 3-act *Metal Slug*–style 2.5D web/mobile side-scrolling shooter about an astronaut fighting a rogue AI satellite constellation | Pure run-and-gun arcade DNA; lacks tactical stealth vision cones, Reflex slow-mo, Codec storytelling, gimmick bosses, and orbital ink aesthetics |
| **Deadbolt** | https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deadbolt_(video_game) | Side-scrolling stealth-action shooter with vents, power-cuts, and lethal combat | Desktop/console only, supernatural setting, no orbital mechanics or MGS-style Codec narrative |
| **UnMetal** | https://store.epicgames.com/p/unmetal | 2D *Metal Gear* homage with gadgets, boss fights, and framing narrative | Top-down (not side-scrolling), PC/console only, comedic parody rather than orbital techno-thriller |
| **Calvino Noir** | https://www.phonearena.com/news/Stealth-games-iphone-ipad-android-phone-tablet_id107160 | Mobile side-scrolling atmospheric stealth adventure | Native app, light combat, no shooter/orbital/boss-gimmick loop |
| **Orbit Defender 3D** | https://www.satellitetracker.in/space-games | Mobile + desktop browser orbital shooter | Pure arcade debris shooter; no stealth, infiltration, or narrative campaign |

**Decision:** Differentiate and build as a standalone project (`projects/2026-11-constellation-zero/`), leaving `2026-07-star-mind` intact.
