# CONSTELLATION ZERO

A mobile-first, browser-playable tactical stealth-action and run-and-gun side-scrolling shooter rendered in Yoji Shinkawa–inspired orbital ink.

Commander Kael Mercer (**ZERO**) launches from compromised Starbase 39-X to breach **AETHER**, a rogue constellation of 144 solar-powered Artificial Intelligence (AI) data-center satellites that cut Earth off while broadcasting the emergency callsign of his missing co-pilot, Lt. Elena Vance (**LARK**).

## Campaign Missions

1. **Get to the Pad** — Storm-lit Starbase 39-X infiltration and launch-gantry breakout. Boss: *Iron Gantry*.
2. **Liftoff & Orbit** — Side-scrolling starship ascent through orbital radar pickets and Kessler debris using **Cold Drift** thermal stealth or full rail-cannon fire. Boss: *Valkyrie-09*.
3. **Through the Array** — Low-Earth Orbit (LEO) Extravehicular Activity (EVA) across linked data-center satellites, power-node hacking, and rescued orbital technicians. Boss: *Relay-03 "Mirror"* (with live Heads-Up Display (HUD) tampering).
4. **The Master Sat** — Multi-deck infiltration of the *AETHER-PRIME* liquid-cooled core superstructure, the Helios-4 truth reveal, and a dual-ending finale. Boss: *Lark // AETHER-PRIME*.

## Controls

- **Mobile (Landscape preferred):** Left floating thumbstick (move / crouch / pitch), right aim-and-fire pad (tap for smart-aim, drag for 360° laser aim), and tactile thumb buttons for **Jump / EVA**, **Close Quarters Combat (CQC) / Hack**, **Electromagnetic Pulse (EMP) / Decoy**, **Thermal-Foil Box** (*Cold Drift* in Mission 2), and **Weapon Swap**.
- **Desktop:** `WASD` / Arrows move & crouch · `Space` jump / EVA thrust / afterburner · `Mouse` aim + `Left Click` fire · `E` / `Right Click` CQC & interact · `Q` or `1–5` swap weapon · `G` EMP grenade · `F` decoy ping · `B` / `Shift` thermal-foil box (or Cold Drift) · `C` Codec radio · `P` / `Esc` pause.
