# Dual goals — initial + follow-on

Every level runs **two stacked objectives**. Clear A to unlock B. Bosses sit in or after B.

Brainstorm defaults elsewhere stay locked (Ash, defense-runaway lore, Pad Reaper / Seraph / Prime, pickup weapons, scrap upgrades, art fork A).

---

## Level 1 — Earth Escape

### Locked

| Phase | Goal | Fun beat |
|-------|------|----------|
| **A** | **Escort the fuel truck to Pad 7** | Rolling Metal Slug convoy: stay near the rig, peel drones/crabs, truck has HP |
| **B** | **Climb the gantry tower · board BLACK FINCH** | Vertical shift after pad arrival; platforms + Pad Reaper on the tower |

### Alternates (parked)

1. Sabotage uplink dish → then escort truck  
2. Rescue 4 techs → then tower climb  
3. Clear fuel trench → then pad assault (no escort)  
4. Hijack Pad Loader Mech → then use it to lift the truck, then climb on foot

## Level 2 — Launch!

### Locked
