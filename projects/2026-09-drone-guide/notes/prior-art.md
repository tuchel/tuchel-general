# Prior art

**Job:** teach a 10-year-old how a quadcopter flies and how to build one, with interactive 3D he can poke at.

Searched September 2026.

| Name | URL | Job | Gap for this reader |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| AirSup rocket-engine guide (the model for this page) | https://www.airsup.ai/rocket-engine | Scroll-driven interactive 3D explainer of a rocket engine | Rockets, not drones; no build path |
| DroneBot Workshop, "How does a Quadcopter Work?" | https://dronebotworkshop.com/how-does-a-quadcopter-work/ | Written explainer with diagrams | Adult reading level; not interactive |
| 3D DECODE, "How Drones Fly" | https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uiCWWF0P7ZQ | 3D-animated video | Watch-only; no controls, no build |
| FPVSIM, "Drone Flying 101" | https://fpvsim.com/drone-flying-101 | Interactive flying tutorial | Teaches stick skills, not why the drone moves |
| STEMbotix / Pitsco drone kits | https://www.stembotix.com/ · https://www.pitsco.com/products/drone-maker-kit | Physical kits with lessons for ages 10+ | Lessons tied to one kit; paid; no free explainer |

**Decision:** differentiate. No free resource combines a manipulable 3D model, cause-and-effect physics (motor speeds → motion), and a build walkthrough at a 10-year-old's reading level. Kits remain the next step after this page (Level 2 on the page).

The AirSup page itself was blocked from the build environment, so the structure follows the general pattern (sticky 3D scene, chapter cards), not a copy of it.
