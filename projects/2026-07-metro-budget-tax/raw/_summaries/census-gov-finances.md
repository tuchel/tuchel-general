# Census ASLGF / Census of Governments — summary for this project

Primary nationwide source for comparable local government revenue, expenditure, debt, and assets using uniform item codes.

**Use:** individual-unit public-use files + GID directory to attribute each local government to a county, then roll counties into OMB CBSAs.

**Coverage caveat:** quinquennial Census of Governments years have the fullest unit coverage; intervening Annual Survey years are samples with imputation. Prefer a CoG finance year for v1 metro choropleths.
