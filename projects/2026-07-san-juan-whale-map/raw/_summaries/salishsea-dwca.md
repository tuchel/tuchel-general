# Summary — SalishSea.io DarwinCore Archive

- **Source:** `https://salishsea.io/dwca/salishsea-occurrences-v1.zip` (nightly)
- **License:** CC BY-NC 4.0
- **Pulled into:** `scripts/build_data.py` → hex density + seasonality for the San Juan bbox

## What it is

Aggregated human observations of Salish Sea marine mammals. Includes SalishSea.io direct entries and Maplify / Whale Alert-style reports (Orca Network / Cascadia pathways). Excludes iNaturalist and Happywhale (those publish via GBIF).

## San Juan bbox slice (this project)

On the 2026-07-31 build: ~2,640 cetacean rows inside `48.30–48.95 N, 123.45–122.55 W`. Dominant taxa: unspecified orca, Southern Resident subspecies (`Orcinus orca ater`), humpback, with smaller Bigg’s (`rectipinnus`), gray, minke, porpoise counts. Month histogram peaks in late summer–early fall for this window (opportunistic effort).

## Use / limits

Excellent climatology layer. Opportunistic and effort-biased; not a survey design. Remarks sometimes carry Orca Network / hydrophone provenance useful for auditing.
