# Prior art

Job: show a public company's reported revenue and its share price on one view, with each figure sourced.

| Tool | Job | Gap |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [TradingView](https://www.tradingview.com/) | Price charts with fundamentals (revenue) as overlay indicators | Generic chrome; no per-figure sourcing |
| [Koyfin](https://www.koyfin.com/) | Charts price against fundamentals, analyst estimates | Paid tiers; dense terminal-style UI |
| [StockAnalysis](https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/spcx/) | Quote, financials tables, analyst consensus | Separate pages for price and revenue |
| [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/SPCX/) | Quote, history, financials | Separate views; no overlay |

Decision: build. James asked for a portal with this as its first view; the incumbents cover the data but not the single sourced, low-chrome view.
